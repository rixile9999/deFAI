### [0:20-0:30] Data Encryption

**"Each municipality encrypts their patient data using homomorphic encryption.**

**This encrypted data is transmitted to upper tiers,**

---

### [0:30-0:40] Blind Aggregation

**"Provincial governments perform 'blind aggregation' on the encrypted data.**

**They can only compute aggregate statistics,**

**without ever seeing the actual patient information."**

---

### [0:40-0:50] Central Analysis

**"Only the central government holds the secret key for decryption.**

**Using Difference-in-Differences analysis,**

**we found that the policy reduced infections by 71.2%."**

### [0:50-1:00] Conclusion

**"This system demonstrates that we can achieve rigorous privacy protection,**

**while obtaining statistically significant and accurate policy insights.**

## 📝 Recording Tips

### Pacing
- Speak clearly and at moderate pace
- Pause briefly at section breaks
- Emphasize key numbers: "71.2%", "3-tier", "p < 0.001"

### Tone
- Professional but accessible
- Confident in technical capability
- Emphasize "simulated data" clearly

### Key Phrases to Emphasize
- **"Simulated data from an Agent-Based Model"** (important disclaimer)
- **"Blind aggregation"** (unique feature)
- **"71.2% reduction"** (main result)
- **"Statistically significant"** (credibility)
- **"Privacy protection"** (main value proposition)
